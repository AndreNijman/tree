Ranged weapons

From Terraria Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
  
This is the main page whose information applies to the Desktop, Console, and Mobile versions of Terraria.
For the differences of this information on Old-gen console and 3DS, see Legacy:Ranged weapons.
The Minishark, a ranged weapon, in use.
For a sortable list of all available ranged weapons, along with their stats for comparison, see List of ranged weapons.

Ranged weapons are weapons dealing ranged damage that mostly require ammunition to be fired. Ranged weapons are categorized into bows, repeaters, guns, launchers, and consumable weapons. There are several other ranged weapons that do not fit into any of these types; all of these weapons, together with the launchers, are buffed by the Shroomite Helmet and referred to as Specialist Weapons in the code.

Due to the usage of ammunition alongside the weapon, the total damage and knockback values in effect during use are actually the sum of the weapon's stats combined with those of the ammunition type used. A ranged weapon with a knockback value of zero, for example, will still usually apply some knockback, since nearly all ammunition types have their own knockback values.

There are 113 ranged weapons.

Consumables

These ranged weapons are unique in that they disappear upon use, and their ammunition count is determined by the quantity on hand. They benefit from ammo conservation, which grants them a chance not to be consumed when used. However, this is limited to ammo conservation from armor items – the Magic Quiver, Ammo Box, and Ammo Reservation Potion have no effect on these weapons.
