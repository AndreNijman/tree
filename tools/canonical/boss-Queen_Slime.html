 “The sovereign of all that jiggles.” 

Queen Slime is an early-Hardmode, Hallow-themed boss. Fighting Queen Slime is not required for game progression – much like her pre-Hardmode counterpart, King Slime – but her defeat may reward the player with useful items.

While Queen Slime is alive, her exclusive music Queen Slime will play. When Otherworldly music is enabled, the track Boss 2 (Otherworldly) will play instead.

Summoning

Queen Slime is summoned by using a Gelatin Crystal in The Hallow or Underground Hallow, at any time.

Behavior

The Queen Slime fight consists of two phases. In the first phase she behaves somewhat similarly to King Slime, hopping towards the player to deal contact damage (albeitmore quickly than King Slime) and teleporting to the player's location when unable to reach them. However, she also two new attacks, where she tries to crush the player with a Queenly Smash, or fires a spread of bouncy Regal Gel projectiles. Additionally, she summons slime minions to aid her in the fight. Upon reaching 50% of her maximum health (9,000 / 14,400 / 18,360), she grows large wings and begins flying above the player while passing through blocks. She can still summon slime minions in this phase and use her Queenly Smash and Regal Gel attacks, albeit in a different manner, but will no longer teleport.

If the player dies or if the horizontal distance of the player from Queen Slime is greater than 500 tiles, she begins moving away from the player (by jumping in first phase or flying in second phase) and despawns in 0.17 seconds once she is off-screen.[3]

First phase

Main attacks

Queen Slime has three main attacks which are chosen randomly, meaning she may use the same attack several times in a row or alternate between different attacks. After finishing each attack, she stays still for 1 second before starting her next attack.[3]

  • Quick Jumps: Queen Slime performs a series of four quick jumps towards the player with varying heights. She stays on ground for 0.17 seconds after the first, second and third jumps, however this is reduced to 0.08 seconds when she is below 66% of her maximum health (11,880 / 19,008 / 24,235). Queen Slime performs these jumps in a set pattern:
  1. Performs a normal jump, reaching a maximum height of 6.42 tiles.
  2. Performs another normal jump.
  3. Performs a low jump, reaching a maximum height of 3.56 tiles.
  4. Performs a high jump, reaching a maximum height of 17.2 tiles.
  • Regal Gel: Queen Slime stays still and charges energy within her crystal for 1 second, before firing a spread of six Regal Gel projectiles that are affected by gravity, and can bounce off solid blocks once before dissipating. The first projectile is fired directly to right of the Queen Slime, and the next ones are each rotated 36° counter clockwise.
  • Queenly Smash: Queen Slime stays still for 0.5 seconds, then leaps toward the player while passing through blocks and attempts to position herself above them, with the exact height and orientation of the leap varying based on her distance to the player. 0.5 seconds after her leap, she briefly pauses midair for another 0.5 seconds, and then slams directly downwards, creating a shockwave upon contact with solid blocks that deals damage within a 30×30-tile square area centered on the point of impact.

Teleporting

Queen Slime can teleport to the player under the following two circumstances:[3]

  • The line of sight between Queen Slime and the player is obstructed by solid blocks.
  • The player's feet are more than 20 tiles below or above the top of Queen Slime's hitbox.

As soon as either of these conditions is met, a counter begins increasing at a rate of 1.5 per second, starting from 0. If neither of the two conditions is met, the counter will instead decrease at a rate of 1 per second (down to a minimum of 0). When the counter reaches 500, Queen Slime teleports to the player's location. If it reaches 0 while Queen Slime is jumping, the teleportation is delayed until she lands on the ground. Afterwards, the counter resets back to 0.

In order to teleport to the player, Queen Slime tries to find a suitable target location with the following steps:[4]

  1. If the total distance between Queen Slime and the player is greater than 125 tiles, she will teleport directly on top of the player, even if they are midair.
  2. Otherwise, she tries to find a valid block to teleport on that is 7–10 tiles (Chebyshev distance) away from the player's center.[5]
    • A block is considered valid for teleporting if it is an unactuated solid block (including platforms, but not planter boxes), does not contain lava, and if the tile above it is not occupied by any unactuated, unsloped solid blocks (including platforms and planter boxes). Additionally, Queen Slime is required to have an unobstructed line of sight to the player after teleporting there.[6]
  3. If the previous step fails, then she tries to find a valid block to teleport on that is 2–6 tiles (Chebyshev distance) away from the player's center.
  4. If all of the previous steps fail, then she will teleport directly on top of the player, even if they are midair.

Once the target location is chosen, Queen Slime stays still, disappears over the course of 1 second, and then reappears at the target location over the course of 0.5 seconds. She immediately starts a random main attack after teleporting.[3]

Spawning slimes

Queen Slime can spawn 1–2 slime minions from her body upon taking damage, with the type of each slime being randomly selected out of the following 3 options:

  • Crystal Slimes, which fire one / three gravity-affected crystal spike projectiles at the player every 0.83 seconds / 0.67 seconds. However, its Expert Mode fire rate is used only if there are fewer than 4 other Crystal Slimes alive. In Classic Mode the crystal spikes are aimed at the player, while in Expert Mode they are shot in tall, vertical arcs above the Crystal Slime with lower horizontal range compared to those fired by Spiked Ice Slimes, Spiked Jungle Slimes, and Spiked Slimes.[7]
  • Bouncy Slimes, which fire pink bouncy gel projectiles at the player once every 0.67 seconds / 0.5 seconds that can bounce off blocks twice before dissipating.[8]
  • Heavenly Slimes, which fly towards the player in an attempt to deal contact damage, similarly to a Slimer.

At the beginning of the fight, Queen Slime can spawn slimes upon taking any hit that brings her below 98% health. Afterwards, she needs to lose at least 2% of her maximum health (360 / 576 / 734 health) compared to her health at the last time she spawned slimes before she can spawn them again, with this process repeating. For example, bringing her to exactly 17,639 / 28,223 / 35,984 (98%) health causes her to spawn slimes for the first time, after which she will not spawn new slimes until brought to below 17,279 / 27,647 / 35,250 (96%) health.[3]

As a result, Queen Slime has a maximum of 24 opportunities to spawn slimes in her first phase, meaning 24-48 slimes in total, or 36 slimes on average. This number will be lower if any of Queen Slime's slime-spawning opportunities are skipped by reducing her health by more than 2% of her maximum health in a single hit.

Second phase

Main attacks

When Queen Slime reaches 50% of her maximum health (9,000 / 700 / 18,360), she grows a pair of large wings and begins to fly. She attempts to stay 15 tiles above the player, and gains the ability to pass through blocks. Queen Slime can no longer jump or teleport in this phase, but can still use her Regal Gel and Queenly Smash attacks in a different manner, which are chosen randomly. After finishing each attack, she waits for 2 seconds before starting her next attack.[3]

  • Regal Gel: Queen Slime charges energy within her crystal for 1 second, before firing a spread of ten Regal Gel projectiles that are affected by gravity, and can bounce off solid blocks once before dissipating. The first projectile is fired directly to right of the Queen Slime, and the next ones are each rotated 36° counter clockwise.
  • Queenly Smash: Queen Slime pauses in place for 0.17 seconds, then slams directly downwards, creating a shockwave upon contact with solid blocks that deals damage within a 30×30-tile square area centered on the point of impact. If she falls for more than 2 seconds without hitting solid blocks, she will cancel the attack early. This attack can be only performed if she is above the player and the horizontal distance between player's center[5] and Queen Slime's center is less than 15.625 tiles, otherwise she defaults back to the Regal Gel attack.

Spawning slimes

Similar to the first phase, Queen Slime can spawn 1-2 slime minions from her body upon taking damage, which can be either Crystal Slimes, Bouncy Slimes, or Heavenly Slimes. However, she only needs to lose 1.5% of her maximum health (270 / 432 / 550 health) compared to her health at the last time she summoned slimes before she can spawn them again.[3]

As a result, Queen Slime has a maximum of 33 opportunities to spawn slimes in her second phase, meaning 33-66 slimes in total, or 49 slimes on average. This number will be lower if any of Queen Slime's slime-spawning opportunities are skipped by reducing her health by more than 1.5% of her maximum health in a single hit.

Aftermath

  • If King Slime has already been defeated, the Princess will begin selling Royal Romance.

Notes

  • The Royal Gel does not make players immune to the slimes she summons.
  • Despite being a Hardmode boss, developer items cannot be obtained from Queen Slime's Treasure Bag.
  • While the boss needs to be summoned in The Hallow, the fight can take place anywhere, and she does not enrage outside of the biome. This means the player can go into a different biome once the fight starts and the fight will continue as normal.
  • Unlike King Slime, the Queen Slime requires leaving the screen to despawn after killing all players in the world. She may not be able to despawn during her first form if solid blocks prevent her from leaving the screen, and when this happens the fight can be continued after a player respawns.
  • Queen Slime herself and Regal Gel projectiles are slowed down by liquids. In the Queen's second phase, she is no longer affected by liquids unless she is trying to stomp the player.
  • The Bouncy Slimes and Crystal Slimes will not fire projectiles while they are floating on liquids, like most other slimes.
  • In worlds with the For the Worthy seed active, the Queen Slime receives the following AI changes:
    • Her horizontal speed during low and normal jumps is increased, allowing her to cover more distance with jumps (low jumps: 23 mph → 37 mph, normal jumps: 20 mph → 37 mph).
    • She fires 15 Regal Gel projectiles in her second phase instead of 10. Additionally, the spacing between projectiles in both phases is reduced from 36° to 24°.
    • She no longer pauses for a short time before slamming down during her Queenly Smash attack.
    • When slamming down during her Queenly Smash attack, her acceleration is increased by 100% and her top falling speed is increased by 14.2%.
  • Defeating Queen Slime is not required for the Slayer of Worlds achievement.

Tips

For more elaborate strategies on defeating Queen Slime, including weapon and arena recommendations, see Guide:Queen Slime strategies.
  • The Dart Pistol or Dart Rifle combined with Crystal Darts can make quick work of her minions as well as damage her multiple times per dart.
  • Palladium armor can be useful due to the Rapid Healing buff it provides.
  • Queen Slime herself has a relatively simple attack pattern. Half the threat comes from the minions she spawns, which can fill the screen with projectiles and quickly overwhelm the player. Killing or avoiding these should be a top priority.
  • Stay in the air using wings or mounts during her first phase, as many of her attacks cannot reach the player there. During her second phase, the player should stay closer to the ground while occasionally luring her back up, as they can avoid many of her projectiles and minions due to their slow and predictable movement patterns.
  • In Master Mode, The Black Spot mount allows the player to hover above all attacks in the first phase.
  • Fight Queen Slime in daylight, since her attacks may be hard to see at night.
  • Queen Slime can be killed easily before any other Hardmode bosses, and even before destroying Demon or Crimson Altars. The Crystal Assassin armor she drops will help the player survive in early Hardmode.
  • Like most bosses, she is immune to lava, but her minions are not. An arena made of lava paired with Lava Waders or Terraspark Boots can make the fight significantly easier.
  • Another effective strategy against her minions is to try to inflict multiple damaging debuffs at once. A good example of this is using the Shadowflame Knife paired with Frost armor and a Flask of Cursed Flames, as the three debuffs are often enough to finish off any of the slimes, even if only one knife hits.
  • For magic users, the Life Drain is a very good choice for this fight. Because it deals continuous damage around the cursor, this allows the player to consistently damage the boss while focusing on dodging. Since the minions spawn from the center of the boss, it will deal some damage to them right as they spawn. In addition, the player gains a boost to their life regeneration while damaging an enemy.
  • Use several disconnected platforms, which can allow her minions to fall down and away from the fight.
  • Any wings paired with flight-boosting accessories and the Shield of Cthulhu (for its dash ability) makes dodging most of her attacks easy (but does not take care of her minions).
  • The Daedalus Stormbow paired with Holy Arrows and summoned minions can take care of her minions.
  • When fighting her in higher difficulties, never remain directly above multiple Crystal Slimes. Their spikes can kill the player in seconds.
  • Invisibility Potions can be used to decrease spawn rates of other enemies and prevent the Heavenly Slimes from chasing after the player. Note that taking any damage will instantly cancel the buff, however.

Trivia

  • Queen Slime's AI was created entirely by developer Grox The Great[9], while the visual effect was created by Yoraiz0r.Verify
  • The feather covered in pink slime on her trophy resembles the sword covered in slime from King Slime's trophy.
  • Queen Slime was one of the two bosses added in the 1.4 update, along with the Empress of Light. Both are Hallow-themed.
    • The Queen is centered on the Hallow's crystal theme while the Empress composes the Hallow's rainbow theme, as seen by the colors of their summon items, their attacks, and the exclusive items they drop.
  • Queen Slime is one of the three bosses whose map icon does not change upon entering their second form, the others being the Brain of Cthulhu and the Santa-NK1.
  • Most of Queen Slime's drops are upgraded versions of King Slime's drops. Both the Ninja armor and Crystal Assassin armor are feudal Japanese-themed sets that do not favor a specific class. Each boss also drops a mount-summoning item (Slimy Saddle and Gelatinous Pillion), a hook (Slime Hook and Hook of Dissonance), and a novelty weapon (Slime Gun and Sparkle Slime Balloon).
    • Unlike most boss loot drops, the Hook of Dissonance's drop chance is not affected by luck, and so will always drop with a 1/3 (33.33%) chance. This might be because the Hook of Dissonance is Queen Slime's counterpart to King Slime's Slime Hook: The latter is also unaffected by luck, because if the Slime Hook does not drop, the Slime Gun will drop.[2]
    • The Volatile Gelatin also shares a resemblance to the Royal Gel, both being the Expert Mode drops for Queen Slime and King Slime respectively and being crowns similar to that of the respective bosses covered in gel. However, this is purely an aesthetic difference and the two have completely different uses.
  • The wings of her second form resemble an oversized pair of Angel Wings.
  • The Queen Slime's crown gore that appears upon her death is different from the King Slime's crown gore. While the King's crown appears when Blood and Gore is on, the Queen's does not.
    • This is likely because not only does her crown drop on death, but her wings also drop.
  • The Bestiary entry for Queen Slime: "Hallowed slimes consolidated into a haughty, crushing force adorned in dazzling crystals. She is rumored to grow wings."
