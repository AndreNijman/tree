Melee weapons

From Terraria Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
  
This is the main page whose information applies to the Desktop, Console, and Mobile versions of Terraria.
For the differences of this information on Old-gen console and 3DS, see Legacy:Melee weapons.
The Volcano, a melee weapon, in use.
For a sortable list of all available melee weapons, along with their stats for comparison, see List of melee weapons.

Melee weapons are weapons dealing melee damage that do not consume ammunition or mana upon use. Melee weapons are categorized into swords, spears, flails, boomerangs, and yoyos. There are several other melee weapons that do not fit into any of these types.

Tools such as pickaxes, axes, and hammers inflict melee damage and can be used as melee weapons, but many are far weaker than actual melee weapons of corresponding tiers. As such, they are usually not considered melee weapons.

There are 195 melee weapons.
