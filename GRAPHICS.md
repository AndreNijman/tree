# Pixel-art graphics pass

Local, uncommitted graphics work. The earlier Phase-2 catalog decisions remain paused.

## Visual changes

- Connected terrain textures, exposed-edge outlines, grass tufts, flowers, ore clusters, and distinct snow/ice/biome palettes. Interior terrain no longer has a bevel around every tile.
- Original broadleaf crowns and snow-covered, layered pines. Sprites are derived from intact live tile footprints; mining or felling a tree invalidates the replacement artwork without changing world data.
- Three cached parallax landscape layers, biome-specific silhouettes, pixel clouds, sun/moon artwork, night palettes, and cave silhouettes. Clouds now remain in the surface camera's sky instead of disappearing above the viewport.
- Side-facing, six-frame player sprites with equipment, nine hair palettes, and working dye shading. Distinct townsfolk costumes plus pixel slimes, zombies, skeletons, Demon Eyes, and cave bats.
- Native-resolution item icons, recognizable boots/phones/mirrors/shields/wings, metal bars, and transparent furniture sprites. Special crafting-station recipes display their station rather than their placeholder material result.
- Consistent blue inventory/HUD chrome and original pixel hearts/mana stars. Mana stars have a correctly sized separate row.

## Implementation

- `js/pixelart.js`: original artwork factories, neighbor-aware terrain variants, live-tree validation, sprite palettes, and caches. No world/inventory/progression writes.
- `js/combatart.js`: bounded original boss/projectile/weapon/arena/troop/general-enemy sprite cache and presentation-only combat draw hooks. All share the 256-entry cache. It is loaded after the base artwork module; unsupported cases explicitly fall back to the retained renderer.
- `js/render.js`: artwork integration, surface foliage tinting, backgrounds, common actors, and light compositing. The combat hook now runs before the separate Etherian-boss branch; the original Old One's Army drawings remain intact for before/after comparisons and unsupported cases.
- `js/oldonesarmy.js`: read-only world/sentry/troop artwork hooks, retaining legacy drawings. The renderer's pre-existing duplicate arena pass was removed; its unrelated duplicate heart-crystal pass is unchanged. Lightning Aura culling includes the decorative field when the device is just offscreen. Troop AI, stats, damage, objectives and rewards are unchanged by the troop batch.
- `js/main.js`: pixel icon presentation and station-recipe artwork only; existing gameplay/audit patches are preserved.
- `css/style.css`, `assets/pixel-heart.svg`, `assets/pixel-star.svg`: in-game chrome and original HUD artwork. Title/settings layouts are retained.
- `index.html`: loads both artwork modules after the renderer's shared color helpers and before game initialization.

No Terraria assets were downloaded or copied. The artwork batches above did not change save formats, network protocols, physics, item acquisition, or world generation. The separately requested controls/combat follow-up below intentionally changes hit detection and adds mining assist.

## Player controls and combat follow-up

- **Ctrl toggles smart mining** for the current player session. Hold left click with a mining tool and aim in a general direction; a gold outline marks the reachable, exposed target. Manual mode keeps exact cursor targeting. Selection respects tool reach/power, tree tools, locked chests, active Eternia stands and intervening solids; water/lava/shimmer are not mining targets. It advances as blocks break but never mines without holding the action button. **Shift remains Run**; Ctrl no longer doubles as sprint. The controls screen and cursor hint explain the toggle.
- **Combat boundaries:** left-facing shortswords no longer reject forward targets through a second facing-sign multiplication. Melee checks actual body edges against a forward stab or swing sector instead of requiring enemy centers inside range. Worm bodies can be struck away from the head, once per boss per swing. Beams and ordinary projectile hits use centered body boxes rather than averaged-radius circles. Ordinary projectile movement is swept in short steps through bodies and walls, with first-contact ordering independent of entity-array order; persistent-hit tracking and Etherian target filtering remain intact. Damage values, attack cooldowns, existing instant melee damage timing and simulation body dimensions are retained. Weapon drawing uses the same reach/sweep extent, without the extra shortsword extension offset.
- **Player animation:** slow idle breathing and brief blinking, speed-driven walking/running, separate rising/falling poses, landing compression, raised attack/tool arms and an animated mining tool. The player artwork fits the existing 22x32 body more closely. Wings are drawn only for equipped wing accessories, including Butterfly Wings; backpack items and Cloud in a Bottle do not create wings. Equipped wings fold on the ground and flap in air. Drawing remains read-only. New animation counters are transient; no save-format or network-protocol change is required.
